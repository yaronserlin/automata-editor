Privacy Policy

Automata Editor runs entirely in your browser. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information:

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no server-side processing.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No third-party requests of any kind.

Your diagrams are saved only in your own browser's local storage (localStorage) for autosave, tutorial progress, your accessibility settings, and the recently used symbols in the transition-label picker. This data never leaves your device, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Accessibility Statement

We place great importance on making Automata Editor accessible to people with disabilities and work to align it with the Israeli Standard SI 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 Level AA.

Adjustments made

  • Full keyboard support for every editing action (documented in the README and the in-app help dialog).
  • Semantic HTML structure with ARIA roles, labels, and live regions.
  • Visible focus indicators and dialog focus management.

Known limitations

The canvas-based diagram area is inherently visual; keyboard alternatives are provided for all editing operations, but screen-reader support for the diagram canvas itself is limited.

Contact for accessibility requests

For accessibility questions, requests, or reports, contact yaronserlindev@gmail.com. We are committed to responding within 14 days.
